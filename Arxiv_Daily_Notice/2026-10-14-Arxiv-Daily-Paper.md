 - **Abstract**
 Gaia has substantially expanded the sample of candidate white dwarfs (WDs) in open clusters, but many remain without spectroscopic characterization. We leverage the increasingly extensive archives of large public spectroscopic surveys to identify 62 candidate cluster WDs with available spectra from DESI, LAMOST, and SDSS. A retrograde IMBH with $m_\bullet=2000\,M_\odot$, relative inclination $\theta_\bullet=150^\circ$, and initial semi-major axis $a_\bullet=0.15$~pc breaks the disc into three disc-like components and an angular momentum filament.  - **Abstract**
 TESS light curves encode a star's pulsation, rotation, binarity and oscillations, among other physical properties, but the survey produces far more light curves than can be labelled by hand. Astronomers have engineered a few dozen physically motivated features from them, which together remain a strong baseline. Previous work has trained encoders on light curves and judged them by their own downstream scores, but has not asked what they add to the engineered features. In this work we take a hybrid approach, initializing the fields in the Bunch-Davies vacuum; and then imposing time evolution through direct numerical integration. Our method efficiently generates realizations of the inverse decay primordial curvature perturbations for axion inflation---an inflationary model in which a pseudoscalar inflaton field couples to a $U(1)$ gauge field via a Chern-Simons interaction. Depending on the choice of parameter $\xi\in\{2, 2.5, 3\}$, which controls gauge field particle production in this model, our method generates realizations with an average runtime of a few hours on 1 CPU core. We compute the power spectrum and bispectrum from these realizations and find a surprisingly large discrepancy between our result and previous work that used an approximation for the gauge field mode functions---up to a factor of $\sim4$ difference for the bispectrum in the case of $\xi=2$. This new method enables not only rapid computation of higher order polyspectra, but also generation of initial conditions for large-scale structure analyses.

 - **Abstract**

#### Seeing Double: The Theory Defying Twin Flares of TDE 2022dbl
 - **Authors:** Xinze Guo, Ryan Chornock, Raffaella Margutti, Yuhan Yao, Erica Hammerstein, Natalie LeBaron, S. Bradley Cenko, Matthew J. Graham, Dina Ibrahimzade, Itai Sfaradi, Jean Somalwar, Robert Stein, Eli Wiston, Joahan Castaneda Jaimes, Mansi M. Kasliwal, Russ R. Laher
 - **Subjects:** Subjects:

 - **Arxiv link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18840

 - **Pdf link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.18840

 - **Abstract**
 Partial tidal disruption events, in which a star survives a close passage by a supermassive black hole and may be disrupted again on a subsequent encounter, provide a unique probe of supermassive black hole properties and accretion physics. We examine TDE 2022dbl, a partial tidal disruption event candidate that exhibits two separate UV/optical flares, using 11 new optical spectra, updated UV/optical photometry, and a Keck-II/ESI host-galaxy spectrum. We trace the evolution of the prominent blended N III + He II feature across both flares and find that the blend evolves from N III-dominated to He II-dominated emission, which may reflect changing ionization conditions and a declining efficiency of Bowen fluorescence. The two flares show similar thermal evolution and spectral features, supporting a partial-disruption scenario. The second flare exhibits a broader, plateau-like peak, a longer decline timescale, and less extreme line velocities than the first flare. We suggest that these differences arise from the interaction between debris from the second disruption and an accretion disk formed during the first flare. From the host-galaxy velocity dispersion, we infer a black hole mass of $\log_{10}(M_{\rm BH}/M_\odot)=6.01\pm0.45$. No third flare has been detected at the expected recurrence epoch, leading us to propose a new interpretation in which a positive-energy kick during ballistic core reformation delays the return of the surviving remnant.
